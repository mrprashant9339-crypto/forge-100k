# Asset Sourcing and Licence Policy

- Prefer original procedural geometry, commissioned work and assets with explicit redistribution rights.
- Record source URL, creator, exact licence version, attribution requirements, download date and checksum for every external asset.
- Check each asset's licence individually; a platform hosting an asset does not automatically grant redistribution rights.
- CC0 assets may generally be reused without attribution, but retain provenance records.
- Do not bundle standalone third-party assets when the licence prohibits redistribution.
- Never rip, extract or repackage proprietary Free Fire, PUBG, GTA or Minecraft game files, characters, skins, textures, sounds or animations.
- Keep generated outputs and human QA records associated with the asset ID.
